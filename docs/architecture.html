⚡ End-to-End Trade Lifecycle: From Login to Exit

Detailed breakdown of every autonomous phase executed by the engine each trading day.

1

08:50 AM — Automated Daily TOTP Login

`LiveTrader.ensure_connection_state()` triggers `pyotp` silent login. It requests a fresh daily Fyers OAuth2 token, saves the session, and seeds 10 days of historical 1-minute OHLCV candles to warm up indicators before market open.

2

08:50 AM — Pre-Market AI Catalyst Briefing

Concurrently fetches live quotes for Nasdaq (26,180.46), Brent Crude ($93.87), GIFT Nifty (24,329.00), VIX (11.20) and scrapes multi-newspaper RSS feeds. Feeds real context into Gemini 3.6 Flash and dispatches a morning briefing to Telegram.

3

09:15 AM — Live Market Tick Streaming

Subscribes to binary WebSocket feed. `DataManager` processes ticks, computes tick-level Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD), builds 1M and 5M candles, and calculates EMA 20/50, MACD, RSI, and Volatility Regime ratios in real-time.

4

09:25+ AM — Strategy Signal Generation

Scans all 21 strategies. Upon trigger, dynamically resolves the exact In-The-Money (ITM) option strike symbol (e.g. `NSE:NIFTY2681124200CE`), verifies cooldown limits, and submits the order to `PaperTrader`.

5

Execution & Pre-Trade Risk Checks

`PaperTrader` validates the ₹5,000 max daily loss limit, 5-position concurrency limit, and drawdown circuit breaker before filling the trade at live ask/bid with realistic slippage. Order persisted to Supabase PostgreSQL.

6

Stepped Trailing Stop Loss & Tax Deductions

Dynamically ratchets stop loss as profit expands (+20pt, +40pt, +60pt locks). On exit, computes flat ₹20 brokerage, STT (0.10%), GST (18%), exchange turnover fees, and logs itemized tax split to database.
